# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should remain professional, technical, and respectful.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- discussing evidence rather than attacking people;
- accepting that benchmark results can vary by environment;
- documenting uncertainty and limitations;
- giving appropriate credit for external work;
- protecting confidential and personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, deliberate misinformation, publication of secrets or private data, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.
